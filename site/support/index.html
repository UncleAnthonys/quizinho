Quizinho

Support

Quizinho is the football quiz party game for the sofa, the pub or the car. Here's help with the things people ask about most.

Get in touch

Something not working, a wrong answer, or an idea for a question? Email quizinho@littlefortstudios.com. We usually reply within two working days.

For a wrong answer, tell us the question and the player, and we'll check it and fix it.

Playing

How do I start a match?

Tap Start tournament, add your teams (and player names if you like), pick a difficulty and the eras you want, then kick off. The wheel picks each round's game.

Can friends join on their own phones?

Yes. At the start of a match the host phone shows a QR code. Friends scan it with their camera and the game opens in their browser, with no app needed. They pick their name and can buzz, answer and take their turns on their own phone. If someone's phone drops out, their turns come back to the main phone by themselves.

What are the games?

Club Connections, Career Path, Starting XI Bomb, Noughts & Crosses, Bidding War, Under the Radar, Football 501, Top 10 and On Your Head. Each one's rules are shown before it starts.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Solo lets you play any game on your own, scored out of 100, or the Solo Cup (all of them in a row) or Survival. Your best scores are kept for each difficulty.

I'm sure my answer was right

Answers come from public football records and are checked, but mistakes happen, especially with loans and short spells. Some games let you count an answer that was missed. Please email us too, so we can fix it for everyone.

Voice answers

The app didn't hear the name right

Say the player's full name clearly, or tap the box and type instead. Typing always works. Unusual names are hardest. On iPhone the app has been taught every player's name, and in browsers and on Android it listens out for the right answers. Voice gets better over time as we learn which names get misheard.

How do I turn voice on or off?

Go to Settings and switch Voice answers on or off. If you said no to microphone access before, you can allow it in your phone's Settings, under Quizinho (or your browser's site settings).

Purchases

What's free and what do I pay for?

The Warm-Up Cup is free. Unlocking the full game (every game, the whole question database and all difficulties) is a single £2.99 payment. There are no subscriptions and no adverts.

I've changed phones. How do I get my unlock back?

Go to Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or Google Play's order history for Android.
