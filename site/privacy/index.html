Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quizinho is a football quiz and party game made by Little Fort Studios Ltd (company number 17149145, registered in England and Wales; ICO registration ZC180534). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and how long it is kept. It covers the Quizinho apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the browser version that phones use to join a game.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Almost everything stays on your phone.
  • When phones play together, the game is passed between them through a database. It is deleted within 24 hours.
  • We never receive or store recordings of your voice.
  • We never sell or share your information for marketing.

1. Information kept on your device

The app saves these things on your own phone, tablet or browser, and they are never sent to us:

  • your settings (difficulty, eras, sound, voice on or off)
  • team names and player names you type in for a match
  • which questions you have already had, so they don't come round again too soon (when you join someone else's game, this list is shared with that game too: see section 2)
  • your best solo scores
  • whether you have unlocked the full game
  • a random ID for the device, created by the app (it is not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID)

Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes all of this.

2. Playing on more than one phone

If you choose to let other phones join a game, the host phone creates a room with a short code. While the game is on, these things are passed between the phones through Google's Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in the EU (Belgium):

  • the room code
  • team names, and the names chosen by the people who join
  • the questions on screen, the answers given, buzzes and scores
  • the random device ID, so the game knows which phone is which
  • times, so buzzes can be judged fairly
  • which questions each joined phone has already had (codes for the questions, nothing about the person), so the game can avoid repeating them; each joined phone also remembers the questions asked in that game

A room is deleted when the host finishes the match. Any room left behind is deleted automatically within 24 hours. Names can be nicknames; please don't type anything you wouldn't say out loud at the table. If nobody joins on another phone, none of this is sent anywhere.

3. Voice answers

You can say answers instead of typing them. This is optional, and the app only asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission when you first use it. You can turn voice off in Settings at any time.

  • iPhone and iPad app: speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device wherever your device supports it.
  • Android app: speech is turned into text by your phone's speech service (normally Google's). This happens on the phone where supported, and otherwise through Google's service.
  • Browser: your browser's own speech service is used. Chrome uses Google's; Safari uses Apple's.

Those services are covered by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies. Quizinho only receives the text and uses it to match a player's name. We never receive, keep or listen to audio.

Helping voice get better: when a spoken answer doesn't match any player, the app may send us:

  • the words that were heard (text only)
  • the right answers to that question
  • which player you then picked by hand, if you did
  • the type of device (iPhone, Android or browser)

This is not linked to your device ID, your name or anything else that identifies you. We use it only to teach the app how names get misheard, and it is deleted after 12 months at the latest.

4. Purchases

The one-off unlock for the full game is paid through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app only receives confirmation that the game is unlocked, so it can restore the unlock on your devices.

5. Football data

The questions are built from public football information, such as Wikidata, Wikipedia and the Premier League's published statistics. This is about footballers' professional careers, not about you, and it is shipped inside the app.

6. Children

Quizinho is a family party game and does not ask anyone for personal information. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The only names in the app are the ones typed in for a game; they stay on the device, or for joined phones are deleted within 24 hours, as described above.

7. Who we share information with

Only the service providers that make the features work:

  • Google (Firebase): the multiplayer rooms and voice-miss notes in sections 2 and 3
  • Apple or Google: speech recognition and payments, as above
  • GitHub: hosts the browser version, and receives the usual request information any website does

We don't sell information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting your data

Quizinho (by Little Fort Studios Ltd) has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. To delete your data:

  • On your phone: delete the app, or in a browser clear the site's data. This removes your settings, team and player names, the questions you've had, your scores and the unlock (you can restore the unlock free from Settings → Restore purchase).
  • Multiplayer rooms: nothing to do. A room, with its names, answers and question lists, is deleted when the match ends, and automatically within 24 hours at the latest.
  • Voice notes: the misheard words aren't linked to you or your device, so they can't be picked out per person. They are deleted after 12 months at the latest.
  • Anything else: email quizinho@littlefortstudios.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because Quizinho has no accounts and doesn't link anything it receives to a person, we normally hold nothing that identifies you. Deleting the app removes what is on your device.

If you have any questions, email us at quizinho@littlefortstudios.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes

If the app starts to use information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
